RePatch: Learning Entropy-Guided Patch Structures with Quantized Representations for Time Series Forecasting

RePatch: Learning Entropy-Guided Patch Structures with Quantized Representations for Time Series Forecasting

KDD 2026KDD 2026

提出两阶段预测框架 RePatch，通过熵引导的动态分块、时间交互建模与向量量化，学习紧凑、可迁移且可复用的离散时间表示，并在 14 个真实数据集上展现出领先的预测与跨场景泛化能力。

RePatch combines entropy-guided dynamic patching, temporal interaction modeling, and vector quantization to learn compact, transferable, and reusable discrete representations for time series forecasting.
