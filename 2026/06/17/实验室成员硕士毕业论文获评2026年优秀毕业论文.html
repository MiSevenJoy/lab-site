实验室成员硕士毕业论文获评2026年优秀毕业论文 Lab Member’s Master’s Thesis Named a 2026 Outstanding Graduate Thesis

2026-06-17 获奖荣誉 Award & Honor

近日，实验室2023级硕士研究生周子瑞的毕业论文 《面向城市时空预测的基础模型研究》 被评为 2026年优秀毕业论文，其指导教师周逊教授同时获评优秀毕业论文指导教师。

周子瑞本科毕业于哈尔滨工业大学（深圳）计算机科学与技术专业，硕士期间主要围绕时空数据挖掘、时空预测基础模型开展研究，重点探索面向复杂城市时空数据的统一建模与预测方法。相关研究成果发表于 KDD、AAAI 等国际学术会议。

此次获评优秀毕业论文，体现了实验室围绕城市时空预测基础模型所开展研究工作的积累，以及在时空智能与城市计算方向人才培养和科研训练方面的持续推进。

Recently, the master’s thesis of Zirui Zhou, a 2023 cohort master’s student in our lab, titled “Foundation Models for Urban Spatiotemporal Forecasting,” was selected as an Outstanding Thesis of 2026. His supervisor, Professor Xun Zhou, was also recognized as an Outstanding Thesis Supervisor.

Zirui Zhou received his bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Technology from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. During his master’s studies, his research focused on spatiotemporal data mining and foundation models for spatiotemporal forecasting, with particular emphasis on unified modeling and forecasting methods for complex urban spatiotemporal data. His related research has been published at international conferences including KDD and AAAI.

This recognition reflects the lab’s continued research efforts in foundation models for urban spatiotemporal forecasting, as well as its ongoing commitment to talent development and research training in spatiotemporal intelligence and urban computing.
