实验室两项研究成果被 ICLR 2026 接收

2026-03-01 论文发表 Publication

近日，实验室两项研究成果被 The Fourteenth International Conference on Learning Representations（ICLR 2026） 接收。相关研究分别聚焦无线感知基础模型与隐私保护大语言模型，探索人工智能模型在跨场景泛化和安全高效适配方面的新方法。

论文 “Map as a Prompt: Learning Multi-Modal Spatial-Signal Foundation Models for Cross-scenario Wireless Localization” 聚焦复杂环境下的无线定位问题。

无线定位是自动驾驶、扩展现实和智能制造等 5G/6G 应用的重要基础技术。然而，无线信号容易受到周围环境变化的影响，使得在一个场景中训练得到的模型往往难以直接适应新的场景。

针对这一问题，该研究提出多模态基础模型 SigMap，将无线信号与三维地理空间信息进行联合建模。研究提出 “Map-as-Prompt” 思路，将地图信息作为提示引入模型，使其能够利用不同场景的空间环境信息快速适应新的无线环境。同时，模型通过针对无线信号特性的自适应学习机制，进一步提升无线表征的鲁棒性与跨场景泛化能力。

该工作探索了将地理空间信息作为基础模型先验知识的新方式，为构建具有跨环境适应能力的无线感知与定位模型提供了新的研究方向。

论文 “SecP-Tuning: Efficient Privacy-Preserving Prompt Tuning for Large Language Models via MPC” 聚焦隐私敏感场景下的大语言模型高效调优问题。

在医疗、金融等领域，大语言模型的任务适配往往涉及大量敏感数据。安全多方计算（Secure Multi-party Computation，MPC）可以在不暴露原始数据和模型信息的情况下完成计算，但将其直接应用于大语言模型训练通常会产生较大的计算与通信开销。

针对这一问题，该研究提出 SecP-Tuning，通过将 Forward-only Tuning 与安全多方计算相结合，减少传统模型调优过程中反向传播和优化所带来的隐私计算开销。同时，研究针对大模型中的注意力计算设计了更加适合隐私计算环境的高效机制，从而进一步降低模型调优的复杂度。

SecP-Tuning 构建了一种面向“数据所有者—服务器”交互场景的隐私保护调优方式，在保护用户数据及模型信息的同时兼顾模型适配效率，为大语言模型在医疗、金融等隐私敏感领域的实际部署提供了新的技术路径。

Recently, two research works from our lab were accepted by The Fourteenth International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2026). The studies focus on wireless sensing foundation models and privacy-preserving large language models, exploring new approaches to cross-scenario generalization and secure, efficient model adaptation.

The paper “Map as a Prompt: Learning Multi-Modal Spatial-Signal Foundation Models for Cross-scenario Wireless Localization” focuses on wireless localization in complex environments.

Wireless localization is an important enabling technology for emerging 5G/6G applications such as autonomous driving, extended reality, and smart manufacturing. However, wireless signals are highly sensitive to environmental changes, making models trained in one scenario difficult to directly generalize to new environments.

To address this challenge, the study proposes SigMap, a multi-modal foundation model that jointly models wireless signals and 3D geographic information. The work introduces the “Map-as-Prompt” paradigm, using map information as prompts to help the model efficiently adapt to new wireless environments based on their spatial context. It also incorporates an adaptive learning mechanism tailored to the characteristics of wireless signals to improve representation robustness and cross-scenario generalization.

This work explores a new way of incorporating geographic information as prior knowledge for foundation models, providing a promising direction for building wireless sensing and localization models with stronger adaptability across environments.

The paper “SecP-Tuning: Efficient Privacy-Preserving Prompt Tuning for Large Language Models via MPC” focuses on efficient large language model adaptation in privacy-sensitive scenarios.

In domains such as healthcare and finance, adapting large language models often involves sensitive data. Secure Multi-party Computation (MPC) enables computation without directly exposing raw data or model information, but applying it to large language model tuning can introduce substantial computational and communication overhead.

To address this issue, the study proposes SecP-Tuning, which combines Forward-only Tuning with MPC to reduce the privacy-preserving computation required for conventional backward propagation and optimization. The study also introduces a more efficient attention mechanism tailored to privacy-preserving computation, further reducing the complexity of model tuning.

SecP-Tuning establishes a privacy-preserving tuning paradigm for interactions between data owners and servers, balancing data and model privacy with efficient model adaptation. The framework provides a new technical path for deploying large language models in privacy-sensitive fields such as healthcare and finance.
