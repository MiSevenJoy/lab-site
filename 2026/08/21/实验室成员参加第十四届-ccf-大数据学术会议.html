实验室成员参加第十四届 CCF 大数据学术会议 Lab Members Attend the 14th CCF Conference on Big Data

2026-08-21 学术活动 Academic Activity

近日，周逊教授、黄锐和鲍侃宇参加第十四届 CCF 大数据学术会议（CCF BigData），围绕城市大数据、时空智能等相关研究与来自国内高校及科研机构的专家学者进行了交流。

会议期间，周逊教授作题为 “面向跨场景异质性城市大数据的时空基座模型研究” 的学术报告。报告聚焦复杂城市环境中不同场景之间存在的数据分布差异与空间异质性问题，介绍了实验室在城市时空基座模型方面的近期研究进展，并探讨了如何提升模型在不同城市、不同区域及多样化应用场景中的泛化与适应能力。

此外，黄锐和鲍侃宇在会议 Poster 环节展示了实验室近期的两项研究成果，并围绕相关工作的研究思路、技术方法及应用场景与参会学者进行了深入交流。

Recently, Professor Xun Zhou, Rui Huang, and Kanyu Bao attended the 14th CCF Conference on Big Data (CCF BigData), where they exchanged ideas with researchers from universities and research institutions on topics related to urban big data and spatiotemporal intelligence.

During the conference, Professor Xun Zhou delivered an academic talk titled “Spatiotemporal Foundation Models for Cross-Scenario Heterogeneous Urban Big Data.” The talk focused on data distribution differences and spatial heterogeneity across complex urban scenarios, introduced the lab’s recent progress in urban spatiotemporal foundation models, and discussed how to improve model generalization and adaptability across different cities, regions, and application scenarios.

In addition, Rui Huang and Kanyu Bao presented two recent research works from the lab during the poster sessions. They discussed the research ideas, technical approaches, and potential applications of the works with conference participants.
