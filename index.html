时空大数据智能与计算实验室

Spatiotemporal Big Data Intelligence and Computing Lab

实验室以数据驱动的智能与计算为核心，面向复杂、异构、多模态大数据，围绕人工智能、机器学习、数据分析与挖掘、数据管理等技术及其在垂直领域中的应用开展研究。当前重点方向包括：通用与领域大数据智能体系统、AI4S与科学智能体、时空数据挖掘与时间序列分析、可解释机器学习与推理、强化学习与模仿学习、多模态时空智能与具身智能、AI计算基座（AI Infra）等。研究成果广泛应用于智慧城市与智慧交通、低空经济、共享经济与城市物流、公共安全、环境保护、智慧能源与电力系统智能等领域。

The lab centers on data-driven intelligence and computing, conducting research on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics and mining, data management, and their applications in vertical domains for complex, heterogeneous, and multimodal big data. Current focus areas include general-purpose and domain-specific big-data agent systems, AI for Science (AI4S) and scientific agents, spatiotemporal data mining and time-series analysis, explainable machine learning and reasoning, reinforcement learning and imitation learning, multimodal spatiotemporal intelligence and embodied AI, and AI infrastructure (AI Infra). Its research outcomes are widely applied to smart cities and intelligent transportation, the low-altitude economy, the sharing economy and urban logistics, public safety, environmental protection, smart energy, and intelligent power systems.

实验室研究成果 DSTA4D 被 Pattern Recognition 接收 论文发表 Publication
2026-08-27

近日，实验室研究成果 “DSTA4D: Content-aware Adaptive Decoupling for 4D Point Cloud Video Understanding” 被国际期刊 Pattern Recognition 接收。该工作面向 4D 点云视频理解，提出内容感知的自适应时空建模方法，为智能体理解复杂动态三维环境提供了新的技术思路。Recently, our research work “DSTA4D: Content-aware Adaptive Decoupling for 4D Point Cloud Video Understanding” was accepted by the international journal Pattern Recognition. The work focuses on 4D point cloud video understanding and proposes a content-aware adaptive spatio-temporal modeling approach, providing a new technical perspective for intelligent agents to understand complex dynamic 3D environments.
我们的研究成果被ICDM 2026录用：SHAPE 框架破解隐私混淆轨迹下的到达时间预测难题 论文发表 Publication
2026-08-26

近日，我们团队的研究成果 SHAPE: Spatial Hyperbolic-Embedding Augmented Pretraining for Estimated Time of Arrival on Obfuscated Trajectories 被ICDM 2026接收。该论文针对轨迹隐私混淆场景下的预计到达时间（ETA）预测这一现实难题，提出了一种基于空间双曲嵌入增强预训练的全新方法。Recently, the research results of our team, titled "SHAPE: Spatial Hyperbolic-Embedding Augmented Pretraining for Estimated Time of Arrival on Obfuscated Trajectories", have been accepted by ICDM 2026. This paper addresses the practical challenge of predicting the estimated arrival time (ETA) in scenarios where trajectory privacy is obscured and proposes a novel method based on spatial hyperbolic embedding augmented pre-training.

实验室两项研究成果被 ICLR 2026 接收 论文发表 Publication
2026-03-01

近日，实验室两项研究成果 “Map as a Prompt: Learning Multi-Modal Spatial-Signal Foundation Models for Cross-scenario Wireless Localization” 和 “SecP-Tuning: Efficient Privacy-Preserving Prompt Tuning for Large Language Models via MPC” 被 ICLR 2026 接收。两项工作分别围绕跨场景无线定位基础模型与大语言模型隐私保护高效调优展开，为复杂物理环境中的智能感知和隐私敏感场景下的大模型应用提供了新的技术思路。Recently, two research papers from our lab, “Map as a Prompt: Learning Multi-Modal Spatial-Signal Foundation Models for Cross-scenario Wireless Localization” and “SecP-Tuning: Efficient Privacy-Preserving Prompt Tuning for Large Language Models via MPC,” were accepted by ICLR 2026. The two studies focus on foundation models for cross-scenario wireless localization and efficient privacy-preserving tuning of large language models, respectively, providing new technical approaches for intelligent sensing in complex physical environments and LLM applications in privacy-sensitive scenarios.

郑宇教授作“时空AI：人工智能进入物理世界的基础理论和关键技术”学术报告 学术活动 Academic Activity
