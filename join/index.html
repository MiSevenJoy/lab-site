Join Us

时空大数据智能与计算实验室经费充足，聚焦前沿，科研思路兼顾高水平论文发表与实际应用价值。欢迎对数据挖掘、人工智能，尤其是时空大数据分析挖掘感兴趣的同学加盟我组。

The Spatiotemporal Big Data Intelligence and Computing Lab has sufficient funding and focuses on frontier research, balancing high-quality publications with real-world applications. We welcome students interested in data mining, artificial intelligence, and especially spatiotemporal big data analytics to join us.

除计算机专业外，我组也欢迎数学、通信、电子信息、GIS、交通等专业，具有深度学习和编程基础的同学申请博士。每年招收 2–4 名博士生，3–4 名硕士研究生。同时，欢迎研究方向与数据科学、大数据计算、机器学习与人工智能、计算机视觉、具身智能等方向相关的青年教师、博士后、研究助理加入团队。深圳市与我校提供具有竞争力的科研环境、启动资金与科研资源，具体意向欢迎邮件联系。

In addition to computer science, we welcome applicants from mathematics, communications, electronic information, GIS, and transportation with solid deep learning and programming foundations for PhD positions. We recruit 2–4 PhD students and 3–4 master’s students each year. We also welcome faculty, postdocs, and research assistants working on data science, big data computing, machine learning & AI, computer vision, and embodied intelligence. Shenzhen and HIT provide a competitive research environment, start-up funding, and resources. Please feel free to contact us by email.

招生招聘信息具体如下：

Specific openings are as follows:

1. 博士后、科研助理

1. Postdoc and Research Assistant

招收博士后研究员 1–2 名，研究方向包括数据挖掘、机器学习、大数据计算与人工智能等方向。待遇优厚，提供海外合作和访问机会，常年有效。

具体政策见：https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/job/view/44.html

同时招聘 1 名合同制科研助理（专职 / 兼职均可），要求理工科背景，硕士及以上学历，具有科研项目管理与材料撰写经验。

We are recruiting 1–2 postdoctoral researchers in data mining, machine learning, big data computing, and artificial intelligence. Competitive salary, overseas collaboration and visiting opportunities are available. Open year-round.

Policy details: https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/job/view/44.html

We also recruit 1 contract research assistant (full-time or part-time), with a background in science/engineering, a master’s degree or above, and experience in research project management and proposal writing.

2. 博士生

2. PhD Students

每年招收博士研究生 2–4 名。要求对科研有兴趣、有主动性，编程与数学基础扎实，有相关研究经验者优先。

博士申请方式：

  • 本科直博生：7 月推免夏令营，或 8–9 月推免考核。
  • 申请-考核制：8–10 月秋季招生 / 次年 3–4 月春季招生。
  • 已获硕士学位者：可申请学校快响计划。

可申请博士培养项目：

  • 哈尔滨工业大学统招博士
  • 哈工大–鹏城实验室联合培养博士
  • 哈工大–大湾区大学（东莞）联合培养博士

We recruit 2–4 PhD students each year. Applicants should be interested and proactive in research, with solid programming and math foundations; relevant research experience is preferred.

Application methods:

  • Direct-admission PhD from bachelor’s: July recommendation summer camp, or Aug–Sep recommendation assessment.
  • Application-assessment: Fall admission (Aug–Oct) / Spring admission (Mar–Apr next year).
  • Those with a master’s degree may apply through the university’s fast-track program.

PhD programs available:

  • PhD at Harbin Institute of Technology
  • Joint PhD with HIT and Pengcheng Laboratory
  • Joint PhD with HIT and Great Bay University (Dongguan)

3. Master Students

招收 2026 级硕士研究生 2–3 名，欢迎具有推免资格的同学提前联系，进入实验室参与科研活动。

We recruit 2–3 master’s students for the Class of 2026. Students eligible for recommendation are welcome to contact us early and join lab research activities.

4. Undergraduate Students

欢迎学有余力的本科生参与科研项目，要求数学基础扎实、编程能力强。视科研贡献提供研究补助。

Undergraduates with strong math and programming skills are welcome to join research projects. Research stipends are available based on contribution.
